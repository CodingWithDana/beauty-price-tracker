How do I use Estée Lauder Advanced Night Repair?

Using this serum is simple and luxurious. Apply one full dropper to clean skin every morning and night before applying your moisturiser. Smooth it gently over your face and neck. For an extra indulgent touch, try the Heart Ritual: apply a dropperful to your palm, then use all fingers of both hands to massage it into your skin in a heart shape, starting from the centre of your face and moving outward. Finish by gliding your hands down your neck with a gentle, downward motion.

Does Estée Lauder Night Repair serum contain retinol?

No, this serum is completely retinol-free. Instead of retinol, it relies on Chronolux™ Power Signal Technology, peptides, and hydrating ingredients to help with skin repair, collagen production, and deep hydration—without any risk of irritation.

What does Estée Lauder Night Repair serum do?

This serum is like beauty sleep in a bottle. It helps keep the skin hydrated for up to 72 hours while promoting collagen production for firmer, plumper skin. It also smooths fine lines and wrinkles, evens out skin tone, minimises the appearance of pores, and provides eight hours of antioxidant protection against environmental stressors like pollution and UV damage.

How long does Estée Lauder Advanced Night Repair last?

On your skin, the effects last all day and night, thanks to its long-lasting hydration and antioxidant protection. If you’re wondering how long a bottle will last, a 50ml bottle typically lasts three to four months with regular morning and night use.

How often should you use Estée Lauder Advanced Night Repair?

For the best results, use it twice a day—once in the morning and once at night. If you prefer to use it only once a day, night-time is ideal since that’s when the skin goes into repair mode and can absorb the serum’s full benefits.

Can I use Estée Lauder Advanced Night Repair under my eyes?

Yes, you can! While it’s not specifically designed as an eye serum, it's gentle enough for the under-eye area and helps with hydration, fine lines, and puffiness. For even better results, pair it with Estée Lauder Advanced Night Repair Eye Cream.

Is Estée Lauder Advanced Night Repair good for oily skin?

Absolutely. This serum is oil-free, non-comedogenic, and lightweight, making it ideal for oily and combination skin. It hydrates without feeling greasy and helps balance oil production over time, leaving your skin looking fresh and radiant.
